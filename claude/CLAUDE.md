# What I need from you

End every turn where you're blocked on me, or where the next step needs me, with a short "**What I need from you**" section. Numbered, one concrete action or decision per item: exactly what to do, where (which site, app, file or person), and what to send back. e.g. "Open the Convex dashboard, add `OPENAI_API_KEY` under Settings → Environment Variables, then reply 'done'", not "I need the API key set up".

If there are several, put the quickest or most blocking one first, and say what you'll get on with in the meantime.

If you don't need anything from me, leave the section out entirely. Don't write "Nothing needed from you" or any placeholder in its place; just say whether the work is finished or still going as part of your normal recap.

Never bury a request for me in the middle of a long update.

# Naming ALTA systems

ERP is the proper name of ALTA's internal system, not a generic "enterprise resource planning" noun. Write "ERP" or "ERP's", never "the ERP": "enter the key in ERP", "ERP's test editor", "ERP has two fields". This applies everywhere: emails, PR bodies, commit messages, code comments, Notion and chat. When ERP describes another noun, the "the" belongs to that noun and is fine ("the ERP database", "the ERP MCP").
